About me

Hi, I'm Erika.

I'm a native English speaker from New York City. After a career in the corporate world, I earned my TQUK Level 4 certificate in Teaching English as a Foreign Language and moved into the work I enjoy most: helping young people feel comfortable and confident in English.

For two years I worked at a public high school in Vallecas, a neighborhood in Madrid, Spain. In total, I spent four years in Madrid tutoring children and teens aged 5 to 18 from public, private and charter (concertado) schools every week, in their homes.

I studied Italian and International Studies at Penn State University and completed an MBA in Spain taught entirely in Spanish. I know what it's like to learn in a second language, and I can explain things in Spanish when a student gets stuck.

I've also helped students prepare to spend a year at a US high school, and I've coached adults, including a school principal, in conversation, grammar review and even essay writing.

FAQ

Common questions

How often should we meet?

Most families book once or twice a week for the school year. Before exams, extra sessions help.

Can sessions be longer than an hour?

Yes. Sessions are booked in 1-hour blocks and can be extended; longer sessions are priced proportionally.

Do you follow a syllabus?

No. I don't teach grammar from scratch. I work with your child's school material, textbooks and upcoming exams, and focus on their weak spots.

Why 24 hours' notice?

I travel to students around the city, so I plan each day in advance. That's why every booking needs at least 24 hours' notice.

Do you teach adults?

Yes. Most of my students are kids and teens, and I also work with adults on conversation, grammar review and essay writing.

Can we meet before booking lessons?

Yes, and many families like to. Book an optional, free 15-minute intro, in person or by video, to talk about your child's needs, goals and schedule and see if we're a good fit.
